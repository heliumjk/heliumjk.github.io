Research interests

Roberto Cofresi, PhD

Postdoctoral fellow. Roberto earned a B.Sc. in biochemistry and a Ph.D. in neuroscience from the University of Texas at Austin. Roberto’s doctoral research focused on testing preclinical rodent models of the ability of alcohol use-related cues to trigger behaviors that promote alcohol use and contribute to the risk for relapse after treatment of alcohol addiction. He is interested in adapting promising anti-addiction and anti-relapse interventions identified in preclinical rodent models for use on human subjects and testing those adapted interventions in the preclinical human laboratory.

Casey Kohen

Graduate student. Casey completed his bachelor’s degree in Psychology from the University of Colorado, Boulder, and his master’s degree in Psychology from San Diego State University. His previous work investigated the neuropsychological effects of young adult hazardous drinking behaviors. He is broadly interested in using event-related potentials (ERPs) to understand how neurocogntive processes contribute to the etiology, maintenance, and abatement of substance use disorders.
